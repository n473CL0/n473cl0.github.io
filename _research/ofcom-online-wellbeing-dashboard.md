## What it is

An interactive dashboard built for the 180 Degrees Consulting data science case competition. It explores responses to Ofcom's 2023 Online Wellbeing survey.

## How it works

* The raw Ofcom survey spreadsheet is processed into a tidy table of responses.
* In the sidebar you choose a survey question, a demographic category and the subgroups to compare.
* Results are shown as proportions or counts in grouped bar charts (Altair), with the underlying data table below.
